Outpatient Bottlenecks & Sensory Design

Multi-Month Waitlists & Neuro-Affirming Content Needs

Outpatient specialty referrals carry waiting periods extending up to 2,000+ days. Additionally, NDIS allied health growth requires calming, low-stimulation content tailored for neurodivergent populations.

Day-of-Service Physical Dwell Time

Beyond macro waitlists, physical dwell time on consultation day routinely extends from 45 minutes to over 3.5 hours for a 15-minute appointment, causing missed schooling and parental exhaustion.

Use Case: Interactive waiting room displays and mobile-streamed short-form content lower perceived waiting times and keep children regulated.

NDIS Era & Sensory Design Requirements

Sensory-Friendly

With NDIS driving massive demand in speech pathology and OT, clinics are building "neuro-affirming" waiting rooms. Fast-paced, hyper-stimulating traditional cartoons induce sensory overload in neurodivergent children.

❌ Avoid: Rapid screen cuts, loud brassy audio, geometric visual clutter.
✔ Deliver: Predictable narratives, calm ambient visuals, familiar Captain Starlight tone.

🚀 Virtual Waiting Room Paradigm

Virtual Urgent Care (virtualKIDS) & Healthdirect AI Triage

Services like SCHN's 24/7 virtualKIDS (managed 16,000+ children remotely) and Healthdirect's AI triage engine have permanently shifted care into the home. Healthdirect's AI tool processed 1.5M encounters, dropping ED intent by 12.1% and doubling virtual care uptake.

The Telehealth Content Opportunity:

When families join a Healthdirect Video Call for a GP or paediatrician consult, they wait in a digital waiting room. Licensing Captain Starlight video content directly into the video player eliminates pre-consultation anxiety on family devices before the clinician connects.
