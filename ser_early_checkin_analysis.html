Root cause analysis by hospital

Perth Children's — 115 records — needs investigation

All Perth records in the dataset are from April 2025 only, and every single one is an early check-in (100% of data). Families arrive from 8:31 AM onwards for a 9:30 AM opening. The sheer consistency and concentration in one month strongly suggests the opening time stored in the system (9:30 AM) may be wrong for this period, or the SER was operating on an unapproved earlier schedule. No group visits flagged — these are individual families queuing up.

Royal Children's — 9 records — mixed causes

One Saturday check-in arrived 49 minutes early (12:41 PM for 1:30 PM opening). Weekday check-ins are much closer — most within 20 minutes of the 9:30 AM open. The Saturday outlier warrants a direct follow-up: was the Captain able to open earlier that day?

Sydney Children's — 9 records — minor, likely rounding

One visit is 10 minutes early (Khurshid Zahan, 21/03). The remaining 8 are within 0–3 minutes of the 10:00 AM opening. These are almost certainly families who arrived right as the door opened — the system timestamp records the exact second, so 1-minute "early" check-ins are not a real concern.

Queensland Children's — 6 records — negligible

All 6 are within 4 minutes of the 9:30 AM opening. These are effectively on-time arrivals — families scanned in as the room was being opened. No action needed.

Monash Children's — 4 records — negligible

All 4 records are within 1–2 minutes of the 1:30 PM Saturday opening. Effectively on-time; likely scanned at the door as it opened.

Centenary Hospital — 3 records — test entries + 1 real

Two entries are under the name "Test Test" (23/04/2025, checked in ~8 minutes early). These appear to be system test records and should be deleted or excluded from reporting. The third record (Chrissy Walters, 30/09) is under 1 minute early — negligible.

Westmead Children's — 2 records — negligible

Both check-ins are within 2 minutes of opening time (one Saturday, one Thursday). No concern.
