Facility Architecture & Volume Distribution

Major Hubs vs. Mixed Regional & Private Sectors

While quaternary children's hospitals manage complex inpatient long-stays, the vast "long tail" of paediatric patients are seen at local mixed hospitals and private day-surgery units.

Major Tertiary Hubs

Quaternary Hubs

Sydney Children's Hospitals Network (SCHN), Royal Children's Hospital (RCH) Melbourne, Queensland Children's Hospital (QCH), Perth Children's Hospital.

SCHN Annual Reach: 167,000+ kids
SCHN Outpatients: 1.1 Million consults
RCH Daily ED Presentations: ~250 patients/day
ED Admission Rate: 23% – 26%
💡 Key Characteristic: High-acuity, complex inpatient admissions with established physical Starlight Express Rooms and Planet Starlight broadcast systems.

Mixed Public Hospitals

Regional & Outer Metro

704 public hospitals nationally. Treating both adults and children in shared emergency and outpatient environments.

Paediatric ED Volume: Majority of 1.7M visits
ED Admission Rate: 13% – 17% (Most discharged)
Dedicated Play Staff: Rare / Non-existent
Environment: Adult-centric waiting areas
🎯 Key Characteristic: High volume of short-stay, low-acuity visits. Generally lacks dedicated paediatric spaces or philanthropic infrastructure.

Private Hospital Sector

Elective Surgical

633 private facilities (e.g. Ramsay Health Care with 72 sites, St George Private, Pindara 13-bed paediatric ward).

Primary Focus: Elective Surgery (ENT, Ortho)
Funding Model: Private Health Ins / Self-pay
Key Clinical Friction: Perioperative Fasting & Induction
Commercial Focus: Patient & parental experience
🏥 Key Characteristic: Targeted around pre-operative fasting, anaesthesia mask induction, and postoperative recovery in day surgery bays.
Outpatient Bottlenecks & Sensory Design

Multi-Month Waitlists & Neuro-Affirming Content Needs

Outpatient specialty referrals carry waiting periods extending up to 2,000+ days. Additionally, NDIS allied health growth requires calming, low-stimulation environments tailored for neurodivergent populations.

Day-of-Service Physical Dwell Time

Beyond macro waitlists, physical dwell time on consultation day routinely extends from 45 minutes to over 3.5 hours for a 15-minute appointment, causing missed schooling and parental exhaustion.

Landscape Insight: Outpatient waiting areas represent long, continuous exposure windows where children require regulation and positive engagement.

NDIS Era & Sensory Design Requirements

Sensory-Friendly

With NDIS driving massive demand in speech pathology and OT, clinics prioritize "neuro-affirming" waiting rooms. Fast-paced, hyper-stimulating traditional cartoons induce sensory overload in neurodivergent children.

❌ High-Stimulation: Rapid screen cuts, loud brassy audio, geometric visual clutter.
✔ Low-Sensory: Predictable narratives, calm ambient visuals, familiar gentle tone.

🚀 Virtual Waiting Room Paradigm

Virtual Urgent Care (virtualKIDS) & Healthdirect AI Triage

Services like SCHN's 24/7 virtualKIDS (managed 16,000+ children remotely) and Healthdirect's AI triage engine have permanently shifted care into the home. Healthdirect's AI tool processed 1.5M encounters, dropping ED intent by 12.1% and doubling virtual care uptake.

The Telehealth Interface:

When families join a Healthdirect Video Call for a GP or paediatrician consult, they wait in a digital holding room. This creates a virtual equivalent of physical waiting room dwell time directly on family devices.

Empirical Research Findings

Captain Starlight Clinical & Field Evidence Base

Empirical studies at Queensland Children's Hospital and remote Earbus clinics document the clinical efficacy of Captain Starlight interventions in reducing procedural trauma and improving health screening participation.

👁️

Captains on Call (Retinoblastoma Study - QCH)

Demonstrated significant reduction in anxiety, stress, and pain during traumatic perioperative phases (fasting, dilating drops, mask induction). Reframed recurring procedural trauma into play-based trust over repeated clinical visits.

Key Insight: Non-medicalized play intervention directly supports clinical workflow compliance during acute procedural care.
👂

Remote First Nations Health (Earbus WA & Lowitja NT)

In Goldfields Earbus clinics, Otitis Media referrals dropped from 54% to 22.4%. Captain Starlight presence improved attendance, extended dwell time willingness, and boosted developmental screening participation (e.g. Cherbourg 3rd Birthday Parties).

Key Insight: Culturally welcoming, engaging environments increase community clinic attendance and screening completion rates.
